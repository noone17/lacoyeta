After Meeting You — For Lacoyeta

You asked me to write again.

So here’s what meeting you felt like.

Read on

Some people scare away little things.You make them stay.

5 feet of quiet magic — met in person.

When you said you wanted that curler,

for a second,

I thought about buying it for you.

But then my mind started asking silly questions…

How?

You're far away.

What if you don't like it?

What would you tell at home?

And somewhere between all those thoughts,

I realized something.

I didn't just want to send you gifts.

I wanted to see you.

I've already heard your voice,

your laugh,

your sadness,

your bullying,

your laziness,

your nonstop blabbering…

But I wanted to witness all of it in person.

That's why I came all the way from a long distance,

just to spend a few hours with you.

And honestly?

Even if it was only for two minutes…

I still would've come.

— Sathyan
