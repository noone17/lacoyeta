Chennai to Trichy.

Just to see you.

Read on

Some moments don't need a plan. They just happen, and you're glad they did.

That day finally came.

I traveled from Chennai to Trichy just to see you.

I came to pick you up at the bus stand, and there you were —

waiting for me in the middle of all that noisy afternoon traffic.

Black dress. Headphones. Backpack.

Tiny earrings. Your bangleless hands.

Loose hair dancing in the hot midday wind.

I took you on my bike, and we ended up going to a café —

even though I never really planned any of it.

And somewhere in the middle of the road,

right in front of the café,

with absolutely no surprise planning or dramatic setup…

I just awkwardly pulled the gift from my bike and handed it to you.

When you started opening it,

a part of me wanted to record everything —

your reaction, your smile, your excitement.

But then I remembered something. A Latin phrase:

"Carpe Diem."

Seize the moment.

So instead of watching you through a camera,

I wanted to stay inside that moment with you.

The way you opened the gift…

You didn't tear the wrapping apart.

You unfolded it slowly, layer by layer.

And the expressions on your face — your excitement, your happiness, that quiet little joy — somehow made the entire journey worth it.

— Sathyan
