page 4 · bird sanctuary

You and the birds.

And one very bold ostrich.

Read on

Some people are gentle with everything — birds, fish, tiny creatures. And then a rabbit shows up.

Bird Sanctuary.

We went to the birds park.

The ostrich even bit your hand

while you were trying to feed it leaves.

You got shocked.

Laughed immediately.

Then came the chicks,

the ducks,

the fish…

For a while, I honestly thought you were just made entirely out of softness.

But then came the rabbit.

It was standing close to the fence,

and two little kids nearby were touching it.

You suddenly went —

"Poo da!"

— and scared them away

just so you could see the rabbit properly.

That was the moment I realized — maybe you weren't as soft as I first imagined.

And somehow, that made you feel even more real to me.

Then we walked into the bird cage,

where dozens of tiny birds flew around us without fear.

They kept landing on your hands,

taking seeds gently from your fingers.

The evening felt peaceful —

not too warm, not too cold.

Just soft wind, bird sounds, and you beside me.

Birds kept flying above our heads,

and then one tiny bird suddenly landed on your head

and started pecking at your hair.

You got confused, laughed,

and kept trying to scare it away.

But this time, instead of just watching your reaction…

I captured it.

And somehow, that became one of my favorite moments from that day.

After all that, we ended up having coffee there.

And while dropping you back home,

we crossed the Kauveri river during sunset.

The sky was turning orange.

The wind felt colder.

And everything suddenly became quieter.

Then I dropped you at your home.

That goodbye.

I think I loved that entire day a little too much.

Until the next drive back to you. — Sathyan
