For Lacoyeta

The kind you do not notice until you do.

Some people arrive softly and still change the whole room.

Her name is Lacoyeta

She is just five feet of quiet magic, the kind you do not notice until you do.

The first time I saw her, she stood there in her uniform, curly hair neatly pinned, a genuine smile that did not try too hard, and eyes that never even lifted to meet mine.

I thought she was shy. Soft, distant, unreadable. Like a story that whispers instead of speaking.

But then I heard her voice. Calm, steady, strangely mature, like she had lived more life than she showed.

And then, slowly, she unfolded.

She was not just that quiet girl anymore. She became laughter in the middle of nothing, a little innocent, a little mischievous, and sometimes a complete fraud in the cutest way possible.

And somewhere in all of that, I realized - no expensive perfume could ever compare to the quiet scent of her curly hair.

She proved me wrong in the best way.

Now I see her differently. Not as the girl who looked down that day, but as the one who somehow lifted my world up without even trying.

Lacoyeta, you are not just small and sweet. You are unexpected, and that is what makes you unforgettable.

— In words, Sathyan
